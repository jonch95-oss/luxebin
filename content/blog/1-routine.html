A good routine is less about how many products you own and more about the order you use them in. Five steps, done consistently, will do more for your skin than a shelf full of good intentions.

1. Cleanse

Start with a gentle cleanser that leaves skin comfortable rather than tight. In the evening, a second cleanse helps lift away sunscreen and makeup.

2. Tone or prep

A hydrating toner or essence gives the products that follow a damp surface to sink into. Press it in with your hands rather than rubbing.

3. Treat

This is the place for serums: the concentrated, targeted step. A hydrating serum such as Drunk Elephant B-Hydra suits almost everyone and layers easily under other treatments.

4. Moisturize

Seal everything in with a cream matched to your skin. Lighter textures for daytime, something richer at night. Whipped, ceramide-rich creams like Lala Retro are a comfortable choice for drier skin.

5. Protect, or repair

In the morning, finish with sunscreen, every day. At night, swap it for a targeted extra, such as an overnight lip mask like Laneige Lip Sleeping Mask, so you wake to softer lips.

The simple rule: apply from thinnest to thickest texture, and give each layer a moment before the next.
