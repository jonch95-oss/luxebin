Because every product on Luxebin is sold below retail, all sales are final. We are unable to accept returns or exchanges, including for change of mind, shade or fit.

We know that is a commitment, so we try to make choosing easy: each product page lists the exact shade, size and packaging, and our team is happy to answer questions before you order at support@luxebin.co.

If your order arrives damaged

If an item arrives damaged in transit, email a photo of the item and its packaging to support@luxebin.co within 7 days of delivery, with your order number. We will replace it at no cost. If a replacement is not available, we will refund the item to your original payment method.

If you receive the wrong item

If we send you something other than what you ordered, let us know within 7 days of delivery and we will put it right, including any return shipping.

Cancellations

If you need to cancel, email us as soon as possible. We can cancel orders that have not yet been packed; once an order has shipped it can no longer be cancelled.

Refunds

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method. Depending on your bank or card provider, it can take 5 to 10 business days to appear.

Nothing in this policy limits any rights you have under applicable consumer protection law.
