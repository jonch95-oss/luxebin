This Privacy Policy explains how Luxebin ("we", "us") collects, uses and shares your personal information when you visit or make a purchase from luxebin.co (the "Site").

Information we collect

Information you give us: your name, email address, shipping and billing address, phone number, and order details when you place an order, create an account, contact us or subscribe to our emails. Payment card details are processed by our payment providers and are not stored by us.

Information collected automatically: device and browser information, IP address, pages viewed and how you interact with the Site, collected through cookies and similar technologies.

How we use your information

  • To process and deliver your orders, and to send order and shipping updates.
  • To respond to your questions and provide customer support.
  • To send marketing emails if you have opted in. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email.
  • To detect and prevent fraud, and to keep the Site secure.
  • To understand how the Site is used and to improve it.

Sharing your information

We share information only with service providers who help us run our business, such as our e-commerce platform (Shopify), payment processors, shipping carriers, email marketing provider and analytics tools, and where required by law. We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies

We use cookies to keep your cart, remember your preferences and understand how the Site is used. You can control cookies through your browser settings and through the cookie preferences shown on the Site.

Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to opt out of certain uses such as targeted advertising, and to appeal a decision we make about your request. To make a request, email support@luxebin.co. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

Data retention

We keep order information for as long as needed to fulfil your order and to meet our legal, tax and accounting obligations.

Children

The Site is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be posted on this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@luxebin.co.
