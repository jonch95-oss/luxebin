Where we ship

Luxebin ships to addresses within the United States, including APO/FPO addresses where our carriers allow. We do not ship internationally at this time.

Shipping costs

Orders over $50 (after discounts, before tax) ship free. Orders under $50 are charged a flat shipping rate, shown at checkout before you pay.

Processing and delivery

Orders are packed and shipped from New York. As soon as your order leaves us, you will receive an email with a tracking link. Delivery times depend on the carrier and your location, and the estimate shown at checkout is a guide rather than a guarantee.

Damaged in transit

We pack every order with care, but if something arrives damaged, email a photo of the item and its packaging to support@luxebin.co within 7 days of delivery, with your order number. We will send a replacement, or a refund if the item is no longer in stock.

Lost or undelivered parcels

If your tracking shows no movement for several days, or shows delivered but you have not received it, contact us and we will work with the carrier to resolve it.

Address changes

Please check your address carefully at checkout. If you need to change it, email us as soon as possible. Once an order has shipped we are unable to redirect it.
