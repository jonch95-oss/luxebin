Every product on Luxebin is 100% authentic. Guaranteed. We know that buying prestige beauty below retail can raise a question, so here is exactly how it works.

How we source

We buy surplus, overstock and closeout inventory from established distribution channels in the beauty industry. This is genuine product made by the brand, which reaches us when a retailer or distributor has more stock than it needs, when packaging is updated, or when a shade or size is being phased out.

Luxebin is an independent retailer. We are not affiliated with, or endorsed by, the brands we sell, and we are not an authorized retailer for them. That independence is what lets us offer prices below retail.

Why our prices are lower

Closeout and surplus stock is bought at a discount. We keep our costs low and pass the saving on, which is why each product shows the brand's retail price alongside ours.

What we guarantee

  • Every item is genuine, made by the brand named on the product.
  • Products are new and unused.
  • Where a product was supplied sealed or boxed by the brand, it reaches you that way. Some minis and travel sizes are supplied unboxed, and the product page says so.
  • Packaging can differ from what is currently on the brand's own site when a brand has since refreshed its design.

If something is not right

If you have any concern about an item you received from us, email support@luxebin.co with your order number and a few photos. We will look into it personally and make it right.
