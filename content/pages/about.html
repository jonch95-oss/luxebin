How it works

Beauty brands make more than they sell. Seasonal changes, packaging updates and overstock leave genuine products sitting in warehouses. We buy that stock through established distribution channels, check it, and pass the savings on to you. That is why every price on Luxebin sits below retail, and why our selection changes as new stock arrives.
